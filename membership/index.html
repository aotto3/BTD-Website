What we promise

One letter a month. Everything else happens when a show does.

There are two of us. We would rather make shows than content, so we only promise one thing on a schedule: The Letter, once a month, written by us.

Everything else follows the work. When a show goes on sale, members book first. When an unfinished show needs guests, members get the invitations. When we open a show, members are in the credits. Some months that is a lot. Some months it is a letter.

A room that holds two guests fills fast. That is why every tier books first.

Questions

Before you open the door.

How do I change my amount or leave?

Every receipt has a link to manage your membership, or use this page. You can raise or lower your amount, switch between monthly and yearly, update your card or cancel. Raising it starts right away. Lowering it or cancelling takes effect at the end of the month or year you already paid for, and you keep your benefits until then.

Can I give less than $5?

Yes. Any whole dollar amount from $1 a month helps, and every dollar goes into the shows. Tier benefits start at $5 a month. One thing to know: Stripe keeps about 30¢ of every payment, so small amounts go further if you pay yearly.

How does the head start work?

Before a show goes on public sale, members get an email with a booking link that opens early: 24 hours early for Keyhole members, 72 hours for everyone else. Seats are still first come, first served among members.

How do I use my seats on us?

Inner Room and Door Holder members get a code with each new run. Use it once per membership year for any show with seats left. Seats on us do not roll over.

Where does the money go?

Into the shows: rent for rooms, building sets, paying actors. Stripe, our payment processor, keeps about 3.6% plus 30¢ of each payment. Paying yearly means fewer of those 30¢ fees, which is part of why it costs less.

Is it tax-deductible?

Not right now. Behind the Door is a for-profit company today. We are working toward fiscal sponsorship by a nonprofit. If that happens, we will write to every member about what changes before anything changes.

Do you keep my card details?

No. Payments run through Stripe’s own checkout page; your card never touches our website. We keep your name, email, tier and payment history so we can send The Letter and thank you properly. See our membership terms and privacy note.

Can I give a membership as a gift?

Not through this page yet. Write to us at customerservice@behindthedoorimmersive.com and we will set one up by hand.
