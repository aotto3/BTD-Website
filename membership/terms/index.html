Who you are paying

Memberships are sold by Behind the Door, LLC, a for-profit company in Houston, Texas. Payments are not charitable donations and are not tax-deductible.

Billing

  • You are billed when you join, then every month or year on the same date, until you cancel.
  • Your amount includes any sales tax we owe. If we ever change what an amount opens, we email you at least 30 days before.
  • You choose your own amount, in whole dollars from $1 a month. Yearly billing is ten times your monthly amount. Your amount decides your tier.
  • Raising your amount starts right away; the difference for the rest of your period appears on your next bill. Lowering it or cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for.
  • We do not give partial refunds for unused time. If something went wrong, write to us and we will make it right.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe retries it for about two weeks and emails you. If it still fails, the membership ends. You can rejoin any time.

Benefits

  • The Letter is sent once a month. Head starts, test run invitations, seats, Members’ Night and credits happen when we have shows to book, open or test; some months have none.
  • Seats on us are for one person, for any show with seats left, once per membership year, and do not roll over or transfer. They have no cash value.
  • Credits use the name you gave us at checkout, or none if you left it blank. Write to us to change it.
  • We may change benefits for future periods. If we do, we email you first, and you can cancel before it affects you.

Privacy

  • Stripe handles your card. We never see or store card numbers. Stripe’s privacy policy covers what it keeps.
  • We keep your name, email, the name you want in credits, your tier and your payment history, so we can send benefits and thank you properly. Allen and Chaney can see this list. Our fiscal sponsor may see it if we have one.
  • We use your email for membership mail: The Letter, booking head starts and notices about your membership. Every Letter has an unsubscribe link. We never sell or share the list.
  • If you leave, we keep your payment records as long as tax law requires and remove you from member mailings. Write to us to see or delete what we hold.
