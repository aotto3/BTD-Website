The Archive

Pick up a phone. Hear a stranger’s memory, or leave one of your own.

  • Format

    Installation

  • Phones

    1

  • Where

    Ongoing in Houston, TX, and traveling periodically

  • Premiere

    Voidspace Live, London, 2026

The room holds an old telephone. Lift the handset and you hear a voice: a confession, a memory, a passing thought someone left behind. Then it is your turn to leave something.

The archive keeps growing with every visitor. And the voice that keeps it slowly changes, from an institution, to we, to I.
