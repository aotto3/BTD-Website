About

No stage. No rows of seats. Just real people in a room making choices.

Behind the Door makes theatre that is small, human and personal. From the moment you walk in, you are not watching something happen. You are part of it.

Our actors don't monologue. They converse, they listen, and they push. Each show is built around a choice that only you can make, and it stays with you after you leave.

Intimacy
A few guests at a time, often one on one with an actor.
Agency
The story can't move without you, and your choices carry weight.
Reflection
Shows about how we connect, and what ties us to each other.

Founders

Allen Otto & Chaney Moore

AO

Allen Otto

Co-founder · Director, performer, educator

Allen has worked in performance since 1999 as a musician, actor, director and coach, and serves as a UIL One-Act Play State Adjudicator. He has taught acting and performance across the United States, Canada, Europe and Australia. He is also a trial attorney, Board Certified in Juvenile Law, who trains lawyers nationwide in courtroom storytelling. Since 2022 he has performed in more than 300 shows with Strange Bird Immersive.

CM

Chaney Moore

Co-founder · Actor, director, voice artist

Chaney has been part of Houston's professional theatre scene for nearly two decades and won Broadway World's Best Supporting Actor award. She anchored the Miss Bennet: Christmas at Pemberley trilogy from 2017 to 2025, has voiced characters in more than 60 shows, and has hosted over 400 performances as Madame Daphne with Strange Bird Immersive.
