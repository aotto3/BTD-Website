The company

Behind the Door

Our shows are made for a handful of people at a time, and none of them can begin until you step inside.

Behind the Door is an immersive theatre company in Houston, Texas, founded by Allen Otto and Chaney Moore.

We make small shows for small audiences. Often it is one actor and one guest, close enough to talk. Our actors listen, answer and push, and every show turns on a choice only you can make.

What we are after is what you’re still thinking about the next morning: the people you’re tied to, and whether you lean toward them or away.

Allen and Chaney are also Assistant Producers at Strange Bird Immersive, where between them they have given more than 700 performances.
