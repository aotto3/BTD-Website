The founders

Allen Otto & Chaney Moore

Two Houston artists who would rather talk with you than at you.

AO

Allen Otto

Co-founder · Director · Performer

Allen has been performing since 1999 and coaching since 2001, including as a State Adjudicator for UIL One-Act Play. He teaches truthful storytelling to actors and trial lawyers across the US, Canada, Europe and Australia. He is Board Certified in Juvenile Law, a former Chief Prosecutor for Harris County, and has given more than 300 performances with Strange Bird Immersive.

CM

Chaney Moore

Co-founder · Actor · Voice artist

Chaney has worked on nearly every major Houston stage for almost two decades, winning BroadwayWorld’s Best Supporting Actor. She played Mary Bennett in the Miss Bennett trilogy from 2017 to 2025, has voiced characters in more than 60 shows, and has hosted over 400 performances as Madame Daphne for Strange Bird Immersive.
